How Points Are Worked Out
  • CMSP, new: 100 points, at least 25 of them participation. Up to 75 education points count.
  • CMSP renewal: 25 points from the last 24 months, at least 15 of them participation.
  • AMSP renewal: 10 participation points from the last 24 months.
  • Summit: 4 participation points, once per Summit even if you registered in person and virtually. A registration counts once the event has happened.
  • Webinar or MISMO course: 2 points. The Digital Closing & eMortgage Boot Camp: 3 points. Both count as education for CMSP and as participation for AMSP.
  • MISMO Boot Camp: 50 education points for CMSP, counted once.
  • Workgroup attendance: 3 participation points for each calendar year, January to December, in which you attended at least a third of a workgroup’s meetings, and for the past 12 months on the same rule, from Meeting Trackers. Where most of a group’s meetings are logged, the total is the meetings its schedule says it held; otherwise it’s the meetings logged. Either way a year needs at least 4 meetings, and one-off events don’t count as a workgroup year. Anything missing, add under “Something Missing?” A past year counts toward a renewal if it ended in the last 24 months.
  • Chair or vice chair: 10 participation points a year, from MISMO’s leadership list.
  • You only submit what you need: we pick the activities that meet the requirement with the fewest extra points, the most recent first.
