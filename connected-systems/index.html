Connected Systems

How MISMO’s systems fit together: which system hands what to which, how the data is protected, what each system replaced and what runs on its own. It’s a view-only page; nothing here can be changed.

The Pages

  • Overview: the headline numbers and the System Map. Point at a box to see what it touches; select it for the full story. The area buttons and Find a System narrow the map.
  • Follow the Work: step-by-step stories of how work moves between systems. Pick one and choose Play on the Map to watch it travel across the System Map.
  • Security: each layer that protects the data, and where it lives.
  • What Changed: what each system replaced.
  • On Its Own: the jobs that run on a schedule or the moment something changes.
  • The other pages show what’s still being finished on each system, the notices systems send each other when something changes, any jobs that failed, and who keeps each system running.

On a Phone

The pages are in the bar along the bottom: Map, Stories, On Its Own and Security, with every other page, Search and Dark Mode under More. Turn your phone sideways on the Map and the whole System Map opens full screen: pinch to zoom, drag to move around, double-tap to zoom in or out, and tap a box for its story. Fit shows the whole map again, Key shows what the lines mean, and Back returns to the page. Turn the phone upright to leave the full-screen map, or choose Full Screen on the Map to open it upright.

How Current It Is

Connected Systems is kept in Team HQ. This page picks up every change within about 10 minutes. The date at the foot of the menu (As Of) is the date of the latest map.

Getting Access

Access is its own item in People & Access (Connected Systems), separate from Team HQ. Ask Jonna Critchley. Having it doesn’t give access to Team HQ or anything else.

Search

The search box finds systems and stories. Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) to jump to it, and the backslash key to narrow or widen the menu.

The Back Button

Your browser’s Back button goes to the last page you were on and closes an open panel or list first; it never undoes a change.
