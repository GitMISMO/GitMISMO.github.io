What Needs Doing

Mark Licenses Outstanding

A company executes a Mark License Agreement only if it wants to use the service mark, and its absence never blocks certification, renewal or the renewal clock. It is still a loose end, and one nobody is watching is one nobody closes. Select a row to send it, see where it is in DocuSign, or download the executed copy.

Certification Scripts

The assessment itself. Each row is one audit question with the criterion MISMO applies, the evidence required, and where the script names one, the exact screenshot to capture. Read out of the assessor workbooks by script and checked back against them, so what is here cannot have drifted from the files Falcon works to. Scripts exist for RON, eClosing and eVault renewals. MISMO completes one and sends it to the assessor before the renewal demo is booked — a renewal with a demo already proposed or scheduled and no script on file is on the queue above, not waiting quietly.

Invoices

Raised in Aptify, tracked here. Select a line to open its record.

Companies

Every company MISMO certifies. Select one to change its address, its contacts and its signer, or to make it inactive — saved once and read by every certification it holds. The same records can also be listed one certification at a time.

Assessor Portal

Select a firm and the whole page becomes that firm’s portal — its own masthead, its own sections, and only its own engagements. MISMO’s side of the board is not dimmed, it is gone. An amber edge means work is sitting with that firm, red that a certification script has not been sent.

Certified Counts

Live certifications by program, counted from the records rather than kept by hand.

Select any company program to list the certifications behind its number. AMSP and CMSP are counted from their own registers, so they carry a figure and no list.

New Certifications

Who finished a certification, and when. A first-time certification is a company earning that certification for the first time; a renewal is one they already held. Only first-time ones are shown unless you ask for both, because those are the announcements.

Certification Programs

Every program MISMO runs or intends to run. The process is the same for all of them — apply, agree, pay, be assessed, remediate, be decided on, renew. What differs is which documents are asked for, whether there is a demo, and how long the term and grace are. All of that is configuration, so a new program is set up here rather than built.

The FRAME Practical Exercise Brief

Every FRAME applicant is given the same brief and one product scenario. This is the brief itself, exactly as it is sent — identical in all four documents, checked line by line. Who holds which scenario, and when it went out and came back, is on MISMO’s own assessor portal.

Cost, Expense and Revenue

What a certification costs the company, what MISMO pays the assessor to do the work, and what is left. Every figure below is the sum of what was actually recorded against each certification, not an estimate.

The Price List

What every certification should cost and what the assessor should be paid. Change either figure and the board uses it straight away — on the record, on the invoice it expects, and on any invoice that does not match. Revenue is worked out, not typed: it is Total Cost less Expense.

Adjustments

Added to Total Cost when the rule applies. The assessor is paid the same either way, so the whole adjustment falls to revenue.

Invoices against the Price List

Every invoice the board knows about, checked against what this page says it should have been. An invoice that does not match is not wrong — a discount or a surcharge is a decision someone made — but it should be a decision, not a surprise.

Application Forms and Questionnaires

What each program actually asks an applicant. Transcribed from the source document by script rather than retyped, so what is on screen started out identical to what MISMO wrote. Turn on Edit to change a question — the transcribed original stays on screen beside every change, so you can always see what you moved away from.

Inside the Questionnaire

The Certification Lifecycle

Every state a cycle can be in, what each one shows as Status, Phase and Waiting-On, and which states it may legally move to. This is the same table the system enforces, so the board and the rules cannot drift apart. It is also where the assessment process gets configured as the assessor side is built out.

The Assessor Portal, Configured

Confirmed as the goal. This is the place assessors sign in and carry out the assessment — not a roster MISMO keeps about them. Blueprint §6.3.

Score the Instrument
The assessment itself, done in the system
The pinned instrument as a working surface — each item with its question, acceptance criterion, required evidence, reviewer guidance, the provider’s own answer and last cycle’s result beside it. Pass, fail, N/A or not tested, with comments and evidence attached. The preliminary result is computed from the items, never typed.
Run the Engagement
Demos, artifacts, remediation
Publish demo slots and accept or reschedule requests, upload the recording, request and accept artifacts against the checklist, answer decision threads with the Product Manager, set remediation targets and close findings with evidence, enter the assessor invoice.
See Only Their Own Work
Enforced in code, not policy
Engagements assigned to their firm, and the same company’s earlier engagements in that program. Nothing about other companies, other firms’ engagements, fees charged to the company, or MISMO’s internal notes.
See the Key for Their Own Engagement
Settled September 2026
An assessor on an eVault engagement may see the key for the suite issued on that engagement — which is what already happens, since the reviewed SMART Doc results come back from the assessor. The grant is per engagement, not per suite: it gives them nothing on another provider being tested on the same suite. The company being certified never sees the key at all.
