How It Works

1Choose the CompanyStart typing and pick it from the list, so Member 360 finds the right record. Its contacts show beside the form.
2Pick What's ChangingSomeone new, new details or roles for someone, or someone who has left.
3Fill In the DetailsName, title, email, phone and roles: Primary, Alternate, Billing, Marketing, Voting Rep and the rest.
4Send to Member 360It saves with your name and the time, and shows In Member 360 once it's on the record, usually within minutes.
