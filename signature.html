New Outlook and Outlook on the Web

  1. Click Copy My Signature above.
  2. Open your signature settings.
  3. Click + New signature, or pick your current one and delete everything in the box.
  4. Click in the box and press Ctrl + V (Mac: Cmd + V).
  5. Under Select default signatures, choose it for new messages and for replies/forwards.
  6. Click Save.

Classic Outlook for Windows

  1. Click Copy My Signature above.
  2. In Outlook, click File, then Options, then Mail, then Signatures…
  3. Click New, type a name, and click OK.
  4. Click in the big box under Edit signature and press Ctrl + V.
  5. On the right, set New messages and Replies/forwards to it.
  6. Click OK.

Outlook for Mac

  1. Click Copy My Signature above.
  2. In Outlook, click Outlook in the top menu, then Settings, then Signatures.
  3. Click +, then click in the box and press Cmd + V.
  4. Name it, close the window, and choose it under Default signatures.
