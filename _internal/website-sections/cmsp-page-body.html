Your Path to CMSP

Seven steps, in order. You do four of them; MISMO does the rest and emails you at each one.

1

Check Your PointsYou

You need 100 points, at least 25 of them participation. Our points calculator adds up what MISMO already has on record for you, and emails you a link to your total.

2

Line Up Your ReferenceYou

Ask someone on MISMO’s Leadership TeamMISMO’s Leadership TeamThe people who lead MISMO’s work: the chairs and vice chairs of its communities of practice, workgroups and councils, and MISMO’s Board. Any of them can be your reference. Not sure who to ask? Email us. whether they’ll be your reference. You’ll add their name and email to your application, and we send them the short form ourselves.

3

ApplyYou

Your points carry over from the calculator. Add your reference, and if your company is a MISMO Champion member, you’ll see how many comps it has left. Your application saves as you go.

4

We VerifyMISMO

We confirm your points and your reference. Points from MISMO’s own records are already checked, so this is quick.

5

Enroll in the ProgramMISMO

Enrollment includes the CMSP exam. If your company is a MISMO Champion member, it gets a set number of complimentary enrollments (comps) each year, and you can use one. After we verify your application, we apply the comp and let your company’s primary contact know; otherwise we email you how to pay.

MISMO MemberNon-Member
CMSP, with the exam$500$900
CMSP for current AMSPs, with the exam$400$800
With your company’s Champion comp$0$0
6

Take the ExamYou

Once you’re enrolled, we open the exam in TopClass and email you how to get in. 150 minutes, open book; 75% or higher passes. If you don’t pass, you have 10 days to take a second exam; after that there’s a six-month wait, and each re-test is $100 for members, $300 for non-members.

7

Get CertifiedMISMO

When your pass is recorded, your certificate arrives by email and you’re listed among current CMSPs. We remind you before it’s time to renew.
