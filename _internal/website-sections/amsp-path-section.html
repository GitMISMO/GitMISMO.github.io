Your Path to AMSP

A shorter path: no reference and no separate exam. Passing the MISMO Boot Camp exam is the proof.

1

Pass the MISMO Boot Camp ExamYou

Take MISMO Boot Camp and pass its exam. Keep your proof of passing; you’ll attach it to your application.

2

ApplyYou

Attach your Boot Camp exam result, and if your company is a MISMO Champion member, you’ll see how many comps it has left.

3

We VerifyMISMO

We confirm your Boot Camp exam.

4

Enroll in the ProgramMISMO

If your company is a MISMO Champion member, it gets a set number of complimentary enrollments (comps) each year, and you can use one. After we verify your application, we apply the comp and let your company’s primary contact know; otherwise we email you how to pay.

MISMO MemberNon-Member
AMSP$200$400
With your company’s Champion comp$0$0
5

Get CertifiedMISMO

Your certificate arrives by email and you’re listed among current AMSPs. We remind you before it’s time to renew.
